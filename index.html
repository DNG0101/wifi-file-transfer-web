Help & connection check
Internet is needed to pair devices, even on the same Wi-Fi. Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs, blocked relays, and sleeping phones can interrupt transfers. Saved blocks let you retry without starting over.
For a 10 GB receiver, desktop Chrome or Edge with a chosen folder is recommended. Browsers without device-folder access can send but cannot receive in direct-save mode. A phone’s background restrictions may stop transfers.

Privacy and browser limits
GitHub Pages serves the application and does not receive your file payload. Peer 1 handles the application's direct transfer connection. Peer 2 exists only while Online is enabled and exchanges small presence records such as UUID, name, Peer 1 ID, heartbeat and version.
Browsers require the receiver to grant permission to a destination folder or download action; after that permission and transfer acceptance, the application saves verified data automatically. Transfer diagnostics/history are not persisted by this page.
