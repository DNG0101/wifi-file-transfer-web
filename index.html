Help & connection check
Internet is needed to pair devices, even on the same Wi-Fi. Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs, blocked relays, and sleeping phones can interrupt transfers. Saved blocks let you retry without starting over.
For a 10 GB receiver, desktop Chrome or Edge with a chosen folder is recommended. Other browsers depend on their storage quota and download support. A phone’s background restrictions may stop transfers.

Privacy and browser limits
GitHub Pages serves the app, never your file uploads. PeerJS handles pairing. STUN helps find direct routes; an operator-configured TURN relay may carry encrypted traffic. The former free PeerJS relay is retired. These services see connection metadata. No global device directory is exposed.
Invitations expire after 10 minutes for new devices. Existing connections may finish. Device names are self-declared; verify who is sending before accepting. Browser storage can be evicted; keep the original file until delivery is verified. This website does not connect to the Android Send Files to TV app.
