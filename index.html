Privacy and browser limits
GitHub Pages serves the application and does not receive your file payload. Peer 1 handles the application's direct transfer connection. Peer 2 exists only while Online is enabled and exchanges small presence records such as UUID, name, Peer 1 ID, heartbeat and version.
Receiving temporarily stores file blocks in this browser, verifies them, and starts a normal download. Download permissions and the final save location are controlled by your browser. Completed temporary copies are cleared on page refresh, or with Downloads saved — clear temporary copies. Your device name is remembered in this browser; transfer history is session-only.
